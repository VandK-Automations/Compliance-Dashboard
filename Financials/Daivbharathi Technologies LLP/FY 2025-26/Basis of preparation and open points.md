# Daivbharathi Technologies LLP – Financial Statements FY 2025-26

## Basis of preparation

Prepared in the presentation format of the signed FY 2024-25 financial statements, from:

* `Daivbharathi_Techn_LLP_Financials_FY_2025-26_Final.pdf` – all current-year figures.
* `Daivbharathi_Technologies_Llp_ITR_5.xlsm` (ITR-5, AY 2026-27) – the authority on classification,
  captions and the break-up of expenses, as instructed.
* The signed FY 2024-25 statements – the previous-year column.

Every figure is carried over exactly as provided. Nothing has been computed, rounded or re-classified.
The statements carry no draft markings, and the Place and Date lines have been removed from both
signature blocks.

## B. Points settled by the ITR-5

1. **Partners' funds presentation.** The ITR carries capital at 90,000, leaves every Reserve and surplus
   line blank, and shows the accumulated loss separately as "Profit and loss A/c (Dr)" 36,13,505. The
   loss is not allocated to the partners' capital accounts. The statements follow this: capital 90,000,
   accumulated loss (36,13,505) under Reserves and surplus. No partner-wise split of the year's loss is
   required, so the "Share of Profit / Loss for the year" column in Note 3A is nil for the current year.
2. **Opening balance of Reserves and surplus.** The ITR is built on the same 11,20,914 opening. It is
   retained. The Rs. 2 difference against the signed FY 2024-25 loss of 11,20,912 stands as a prior-period
   item; changing it would put the accounts out of step with the return.
3. **Classification of the 30,47,564 loan.** The ITR places it under "Unsecured loans (including
   deposits) – Others", which ranks above current liabilities. It is therefore shown as a long-term
   unsecured borrowing, captioned "Loans and advances - Others", in Note 5.
4. **Note 9 cash captions.** The ITR uses "Balances with Banks", "Cash in hand" and "Others" with the
   same three amounts. These captions are kept rather than being renamed to "On current accounts".
5. **Note 7 caption.** "Other payables" is confirmed for the 46,506.
6. **Note 12 expense heads.** The ITR's own break-up uses the same 24 heads, with rougher spellings such
   as "Subscrptions", "Accomodation", "Preincorporation" and "Web Site Development". All 24 are therefore
   kept verbatim, in the statements' spellings, and are not folded into the reference's fixed head names.
   The ITR also treats Conveyance and Professional / consultancy / technical fees as heads in their own
   right, so neither has been merged into Travelling expenses or Legal and professional charges.
7. **Trade payables ageing and the nil lines.** Trade creditors beyond one year, trade debtors, other
   current assets and fixed assets are all blank in the ITR, confirming Note 13 and the nil rows on the
   face of the Balance Sheet.
8. **Partners' remuneration and interest.** Both blank in the ITR, confirming the nil columns in
   Note 3A and item X of the Profit and Loss.

Note that the ITR shows the debit profit and loss balance on the assets side rather than netting it.
That is the return's own layout. The statements net it under Reserves and surplus, which is the correct
presentation for financial statements and is what the FY 2025-26 statements themselves do.

## C. Remaining open points

1. **Related party status of the 30,47,564 loan.** The ITR leaves "Unsecured Rupee Loans from persons
   u/s 40A(2)(b)" blank, which points to the loan not being from a specified related party. Note 2.9 of
   the FY 2025-26 statements makes the opposite, positive statement, listing the same balance as a
   related-party balance. I have kept the disclosure in Note 14, because a blank field in a return is an
   omission rather than a statement, and dropping a required disclosure is the riskier direction. Say the
   word and I will remove that one row.
2. **Partner-wise split of the 90,000 capital.** The ITR balance sheet does not carry it, and Note 2.1(B)
   and (D) of the statements are blank. Reading the two years together, Samartha 90,000 and Manasa nil
   looks right, since FY 2024-25 showed Manasa's 10,000 as a receivable and FY 2025-26 shows 90,000
   received with no receivable. I have not assumed it, so those cells in Note 3A, along with the opening
   and introduced columns, are blank.
3. **Nature of the "Others" 18 in cash.** The caption is confirmed by the ITR but the nature is not
   stated anywhere.
4. **Previous-year comparatives.** The ITR is a single-year return, so it does not speak to this. The
   comparatives are taken from the signed FY 2024-25 statements.
5. **Name spellings.** The reference uses "Samartha Raghava Nagabhushanam I" with a trailing "I",
   "Samartha R N", "Manasa Nagabhushanam" and "Manasa Nagabhushnam" in different places, and "DIN". The
   FY 2025-26 statements use "MANASA NAGABHUSHNAM" and "DPIN". The reference text is replicated in each
   place. The ITR carries no partner names.
6. **Text corrections** – see section D.

## E. Presentation differences from the FY 2025-26 source, and why

* **Notes 13 and 14** carry the trade payables ageing schedule and the related-party disclosures, which
  have no counterpart in the reference format. The MSME 45-day statement sits under Note 6.
* **Omitted**: the nil trade receivables ageing schedule, the partners' rights and duties paragraph, and
  "Earnings per share – Not applicable". None appears in the reference format and all are nil or
  narrative.
* **Note 4** carries the opening, loss and closing movement from Note 2.2 of the FY 2025-26 statements.
  The reference showed only a closing balance. The ITR corroborates the movement.
* **Note 5A** keeps the FY 2024-25 short-term borrowing of 7,80,680 on its own note, since that year
  classified it as short-term while FY 2025-26 classifies its loan as long-term.
